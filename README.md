#Title: Data-Driven Inventory Management and Delivery Optimization for an E-Commerce Logistics Company

##Project Overview

This project is part of the YuvaIntern program and focuses on applying data science techniques to improve inventory management and delivery operations in an e-commerce logistics company.

The project analyses e-commerce logistics activities such as product demand, inventory levels, warehouse performance, delivery time, shipping costs, and order fulfilment. The main objective is to use data-driven methods to identify logistics problems and develop strategies that can improve operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

Project Area

Logistics Data Analysis

##Project Objectives

###The main objectives of this project are:

Analyse historical e-commerce order data.

Identify high-demand and slow-moving products.

Analyse inventory levels and stockout patterns.

Evaluate warehouse performance.

Analyse delivery times and shipping costs.

Forecast future product demand.

Predict potential delivery delays.

Develop inventory replenishment strategies.

Improve warehouse allocation.

Recommend delivery optimization strategies.

##Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following KPIs are considered for evaluating logistics performance:

KPI	Purpose
Inventory Turnover	Measures how efficiently inventory is sold
Stockout Rate	Measures the frequency of inventory shortages
On-Time Delivery Rate	Measures delivery performance
Average Delivery Time	Measures average delivery duration
Average Shipping Cost	Measures transportation cost
Order Fulfilment Rate	Measures the percentage of successfully fulfilled orders

##Data Science Techniques

The project proposes the use of the following techniques:

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) – to understand sales, inventory, and delivery patterns.

Regression – to predict demand or delivery time.

Classification – to identify orders that may experience delays.

Clustering – to group products according to demand patterns.

Time-Series Analysis – to identify trends and seasonal demand.

Optimization – to improve inventory, warehouse, and delivery decisions.

##Week 1 – Strategic Planning

##Expected Outcomes

The project is expected to provide useful insights into inventory and delivery operations. The analysis can help identify products with high demand, stockout risks, warehouse inventory imbalances, delivery delays, and high shipping costs.

The final objective is to support better decision-making by reducing unnecessary inventory, improving product availability, controlling logistics costs, improving delivery performance, and increasing customer satisfaction.

##Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data science and Python can be applied to real-world e-commerce logistics problems. The strategic planning and KPI analysis performed in Week 1 provide the foundation for data cleaning, exploratory analysis, predictive modelling, and optimization in the following stages of the project.
